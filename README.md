# Gotcha Now!
## Abstract
You are gacha character pulled from a loot box. Your goal: Exploit your player with pop-ups, FOMO, and endless buy prompts, or break the system and help them escape their addiction!

Created by: Mauro Zocco, Muriel Ackermann, Benjamin Scherrer

## Context
*Gotcha Now!* is a group project conceived over produced over the the course of the 5th semester of the [Bachelor Major Game Design](https://gamedesign.zhdk.ch/) at [ZHdK](https://www.zhdk.ch/en/zurich-university-of-the-arts-1).

## Overview
*Gotcha Now!* is a multilinear third person action game featuring a unique take on a powerup system to explore the effects of a players descicions on a meta layer. The game addresses and criticises common manipulation tactics of the popular gaming genre *Gacha*. The game uses a two stick control sceme for input with a x-box controller. The player can perform various slash attacks, a parry and a dodge. The various enemy are driven by an elaborate controller, staging various types of attacks depending on the state of the battle to maximize engaged gameplay and tension. Using the popup menu on the bottom left the player can request various powerups from the game system, influencing story events without affecting the immersion.

## Design
*Gotcha Now" combines styles from various inspirations bringing forth a mix of modern graphic design, anime, and retro gaming. 

The game window is subdived in two parts, to the left a 3:4 3d view, and a 16:9 phonescreen to the right. The 3d view displays the main game content and stages the fights. With a VCR filter and low poly models the 3d view gives a retro aestetic, transmitting the feeling of a virtual world. The phone view displays the health bars of the player and the boss enemy. Additionally popup messages and push notifications can appear on the phone screen. The phone screen is deliberately overwhelming to look at, with moments where the player cannot catch all information displayed, mimicking the state of *Gacha* games. 

<img width="2880" height="1800" alt="Screenshot 2026-05-31 190842" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/a6229136-9062-4ffa-ba55-714302a6398f" />

The pastel colors and clear design language help keeping the game soothing to look despite its high grade of detail and visual noise. The base enemy Katoro is designed as a hulking battle droid, mixing the feeling of danger with an endearing clumsyness, framing its limited move set as a characteristic of a typical first genration video game enemy. The heroine Asuka is framed as a typical anime girl, with red hair and exposing clothes and magical rapier. On closer inspection it is evident that she is a parital pallete swap of the main villain Aya. It was import to us to have an emancipated cast, while discusing the sexualisation of video game characters in the context of baiting microtransactions, while remaining tasteful and a ironic. The low poly aestetic removes the effect from the usually sexualizing gestures, while still making the topic apparent to the viewer.

## Narration
The game has three endings, with each of them only achievable by clearing certain conditions. Each use of the powerup system is tracked and contributes to which ending is eventually selected. We follow *Asuka*, a character within a mobile game. She lives within a digital real and is tasked with tricking players into paying micro transactions for the game with powerup requests. Her player, *Bob*, unable to resist the feeling of short term success, always pays up.

<img width="2880" height="1800" alt="Screenshot 2026-05-31 190739" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/ea51abe8-5a83-47a1-a666-e8b972c33265" />

Over the course of the game the audience learns of the consequnces of their actions. Through simulated push notiffications they can follow Bobs social decline and finacial issues due to game addiction. Thanks to the multilinear narration device the players can replay the game and stop finacially exploiting Bob, and help him return to society.

[FMOD](https://www.fmod.com/) is used for dynamic music and mixing.
